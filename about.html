About LURIXA

Illuminate Your World. Furnish Your Life.

Bridging the gap

Factory quality, delivered to UK homes.

LURIXA was founded to bridge the gap between factory-quality home interiors and UK consumers. Our sourcing team travels directly to China — to Guangzhou, Foshan, Zhongshan and Shenzhen — to hand-select products from manufacturers who supply Europe’s leading retailers.

By cutting out the middlemen, we pass the savings directly to you, while maintaining strict UK compliance standards including UKCA certification, RoHS compliance and BS EN safety testing.

What we stand for

Mission, Process & Promise

Our Mission

To make beautifully made, fairly priced lighting and furniture accessible to every UK home — without compromising on quality or compliance.

Our Process

We visit factories in person, inspect materials and finishes, and only bring back pieces that meet our standards for style, durability and value.

Our Promise

Honest pricing, UK-certified products, and dependable nationwide delivery — backed by a team that genuinely cares about your space.
