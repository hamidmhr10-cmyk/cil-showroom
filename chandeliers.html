How to choose a chandelier

A chandelier works hardest as the centrepiece of a room, so scale matters. As a rough guide, choose a fitting that feels in proportion with the room and the furniture beneath it, and check the overall drop against your ceiling height before you order.

Over a dining table, a chandelier usually hangs about 75 cm above the surface so it lights the table without blocking the view across it. In a hallway or stairwell, make sure the lowest point clears head height.
