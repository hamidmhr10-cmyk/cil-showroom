Choosing a ceiling fan with a light

A fan and light combined saves ceiling space and gives you air movement and illumination from a single fitting. They suit bedrooms, living rooms and conservatories.

Check your ceiling height and the fitting’s overall drop before you order, and make sure there is enough clearance around the blades.
