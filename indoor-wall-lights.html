Choosing indoor wall lights

Wall lights add a soft layer of light at eye level, which flatters a room in the evening. A matching pair works well either side of a bed, a mirror or a fireplace.

Check the size and how far each fitting projects from the wall against the space you have, and choose a light colour that suits the room. These fittings are for indoor use only.

You can see pieces in person at our Luton showroom, by appointment. Request a quote or get in touch and we’ll help you choose.
