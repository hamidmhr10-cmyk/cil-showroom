Choosing garden furniture

Measure your patio or garden space and think about how many people you want to seat. Check the materials and finishes listed on each product.

Outdoor furniture lasts longer if you cover it or store it over the winter, so think about where it will live when you are not using it.

You can see pieces in person at our Luton showroom, by appointment. Request a quote or get in touch and we’ll help you choose.
