Choosing a TV unit

Measure the width of your TV, including the stand, and leave a little extra space on each side. Check the dimensions listed on each product.

Storage matters as much as the stand: think about what you need to hide away, and whether you want cable management for consoles and set-top boxes.

You can see pieces in person at our Luton showroom, by appointment. Request a quote or get in touch and we’ll help you choose.
